Restaurento Platform Policies & Frequently Asked Questions

========================================
1. Cancellation Policy
========================================

1a. Who Can Cancel:
- Only the user who made the booking can cancel it from the "My Bookings" section.
- You cannot cancel a booking that has already been checked-in.
- You cannot cancel a booking that is already cancelled.
- You cannot cancel a past booking (a booking whose date and time slot have already passed).

1b. Refund on Cancellation:
- If you cancel a confirmed (approved) booking, the full booking amount is refunded 100% to your Restaurento Wallet — no cancellation fees are charged.
- If you cancel a booking that is still in "pending-payment" status (payment not yet completed), no refund is issued because no payment was collected yet.
- All refunds are credited to your Restaurento Wallet, not back to your original payment method (credit card/bank/UPI).
- You will receive an in-app notification confirming the cancellation and the refund amount.

1c. Restaurant-Initiated Cancellations:
- If a restaurant cancels your booking, you are entitled to a 100% full refund with no fees.
- The full amount is credited to your Restaurento Wallet.
- You will receive an in-app notification immediately when a restaurant cancels your booking.

1d. Special Circumstances:
- For cancellations due to medical emergencies or exceptional circumstances, please contact support@restaurento.com with documentation. These are reviewed case by case.

========================================
2. No-Show Policy
========================================

- If a diner does not arrive within 15 minutes of their scheduled booking time without prior notice, the reservation is marked as a "No-Show".
- No-shows are not eligible for any refund. The full amount paid during booking will be forfeited.
- Repeated no-shows (more than 3 in a month) may result in the suspension of the diner's account.

========================================
3. Wallet and Refund Rules
========================================

- Refunds for cancellations are always credited to the Restaurento Wallet, not back to the original payment source (credit card/bank).
- Wallet balances cannot be withdrawn to a bank account. They can only be used for future bookings on the Restaurento platform.
- When making a new booking, you can choose to apply your wallet balance. If the wallet balance is insufficient, the remaining amount must be paid via Razorpay.
- Your wallet transaction history is available in the "My Wallet" section of the app, showing all credits and debits with timestamps.
- Wallet credits earned from referrals, cancellation refunds, and goodwill gestures all appear in the transaction log.

========================================
4. Pricing, Taxes & Platform Fees
========================================

- All booking prices shown are exclusive of taxes and platform fees.
- A 5% tax (GST) is applied to the booking total at checkout.
- A 5% platform fee is charged on every booking to support the Restaurento platform.
- The final breakdown of slot price, taxes, and platform fees is clearly displayed on the checkout confirmation screen before you pay.
- There are no hidden charges beyond the tax and platform fee.

========================================
5. Payment and Payment Retries
========================================

- All payments are processed securely via Razorpay.
- When you initiate a booking, your table is temporarily held for up to 5 minutes (300 seconds) while you complete payment.
- You have a total window of 10 minutes (600 seconds) to successfully complete a payment before the hold is released.
- If a payment fails during checkout, you have a 2-minute (120 second) window and up to 3 retry attempts to retry the payment.
- If the payment is not completed within the retry window, the table hold is released and you will have to restart the booking process from scratch.
- You can pay using Razorpay (credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking) or by applying your Restaurento Wallet balance.

========================================
6. Coupon Codes
========================================

- Only one coupon code or offer can be applied per booking. They cannot be combined.
- Coupons are percentage-based discounts (e.g., a 20% coupon saves 20% of your total).
- Each coupon has a maximum discount cap. Even if your percentage calculates higher, the maximum saving is capped (up to ₹1000 maximum cap).
- Coupons may require a minimum order value to be eligible. Check the coupon details before applying.
- Coupons have a usage limit (maximum number of times they can be used across all users). Once the limit is hit, the coupon is no longer applicable.
- Coupons have an expiry date. Expired coupons cannot be applied.
- If a booking with an applied coupon is cancelled, the coupon is considered "used" and cannot be applied again to a future booking.
- Coupons are not case-sensitive. "SAVE20" and "save20" are treated as the same code.
- You can view all currently available and valid coupons on the checkout page before completing your booking.

========================================
7. Restaurant Offers
========================================

- Restaurants can create their own flat-discount offers (e.g., "Get ₹50 off on bills above ₹300").
- Restaurant offers are flat amounts in ₹ (not percentages). The maximum offer discount is ₹1000.
- Every offer has a minimum bill threshold. The discount amount must always be strictly less than the minimum bill value. For example, a ₹50 offer requires the bill to be at least ₹51 or more.
- The minimum bill threshold is based on your pre-ordered food total, not the full booking cost.
- Offers have a validity window (valid from and valid until dates). You cannot apply an expired offer.
- Platform coupons and restaurant-specific offers cannot be combined in the same booking.
- Active offers are visible on the restaurant's booking page. Inactive or expired offers will not appear.

========================================
8. Pre-ordering Food
========================================

- Pre-ordered food is confirmed along with the table booking and is paid for at checkout.
- Pre-ordered items are served at your table upon arrival. You do not need to place a separate order at the restaurant.
- If you cancel the booking, the food pre-order is also cancelled automatically under the same cancellation policy terms.
- You cannot modify the pre-ordered food once the booking is confirmed. You must cancel and re-book if you want to change your food order.
- Each food item has a maximum order quantity of 99 per booking.
- Menu items are marked as either Veg 🟢 or Non-Veg 🔴. Please review your pre-order carefully.
- Not all menu items may be available. Unavailable items are hidden from the menu during booking.

========================================
9. General Booking Rules
========================================

- The maximum party size per booking is 20 people. Some restaurants may have lower limits.
- Bookings are non-transferable. The person who made the booking must be present.
- Children under 5 years old do not need to be counted in the party size for table capacity, but please mention them in the booking notes.
- A QR code check-in token is generated for every confirmed booking. This is used by the restaurant staff to verify your arrival.
- The check-in QR code is valid only until the end of your scheduled time slot. It cannot be used after your slot ends.
- You can access your QR code from the "My Bookings" section of the app.

========================================
10. Referral Program
========================================

- Every Restaurento user has a unique referral code visible in their profile.
- When a new user signs up using your referral code, both of you receive a wallet credit reward.
- The referrer (you) earns ₹10 in Restaurento Wallet credit.
- The new user (your friend) earns ₹5 in Restaurento Wallet credit as a welcome bonus.
- Referral rewards are credited to the wallet after the new user successfully verifies their email.
- The referral reward can only be claimed once per account. You cannot refer the same person twice.
- Referral rewards can be used towards any future booking on the platform.

========================================
11. Reviews & Ratings
========================================

- You can leave a review and rating for a restaurant after completing a booking (status: checked-in).
- Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
- You can only submit one review per restaurant. If you have booked the same restaurant multiple times, you can update your existing review.
- Review comments must not exceed 500 characters.
- Restaurento reserves the right to remove reviews that contain abusive language, spam, or false information.

========================================
12. Account Suspension & Bans
========================================

- Accounts may be suspended for: repeated no-shows (more than 3 per month), repeated cancellations (more than 5 per month), abusive behavior, or violation of platform terms.
- Suspended users cannot make new bookings but can still view their booking history and wallet balance.
- If you believe your account was suspended in error, please contact support@restaurento.com for a review.
- Restaurants on the platform are verified by the Restaurento admin team before being listed. They must submit a valid restaurant license, business certificate, FSSAI certificate, and owner ID.

========================================
13. Temporary Restaurant Closures
========================================

- A restaurant may temporarily close due to an emergency, renovation, or maintenance. When this happens, the restaurant sets a "closed until" date.
- If the restaurant chooses to cancel existing bookings during its closure period, every affected booking is automatically cancelled and you receive a 100% full refund to your Restaurento Wallet with no fees.
- You will receive an in-app notification immediately if your booking is cancelled due to a restaurant closure. The notification will state the restaurant name, your booking date, and the closure period.
- If a restaurant is temporarily closed, its booking slots will be unavailable until it reopens.

========================================
14. Restaurant Schedule & Pricing Changes
========================================

- Restaurants may update their seating capacity, slot price, opening hours, and slot duration at any time.
- Due to fairness for existing bookings, any changes to timing, pricing, or seat capacity take effect only after a 5-day lead time. This means your confirmed booking will always honour the price and schedule that was in effect when you booked.
- Minor profile updates such as description, photos, and cuisine tags take effect immediately.
- Restaurants can submit their verification documents up to a maximum of 3 times. After 3 rejected attempts, the account requires admin intervention.

========================================
15. Booking Statuses Explained
========================================

- pending-payment: Your booking has been initiated and a table hold is active, but payment has not been completed yet. This status auto-expires after 30 minutes if payment is not completed.
- approved: Your payment is confirmed and your reservation is secured. You will receive a QR check-in token for this booking.
- checked-in: The restaurant has scanned your QR code and you have been marked as arrived. This is the final successful status.
- cancelled: The booking has been cancelled, either by you or by the restaurant. Refunds (if applicable) are credited to your wallet.

========================================
16. Contact & Support
========================================

- For any policy questions, billing disputes, or account issues, contact our support team at: support@restaurento.com
- Our team typically responds within 24 business hours.
- For urgent issues related to an active or upcoming booking, please mention your Booking ID in the subject line for faster resolution.
